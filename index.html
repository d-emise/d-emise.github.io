// features

01 / aimbot & silent aim

aimbot with fov, smoothing, and bone selection. silent aim redirects your shots to the target.

02 / visuals

customizable 2d boxes, text labels, healthbars, distance metrics, and customizable outline/mesh chams.

03 / lua executor

allows reading and writing virtual memory addresses, traversing the roblox instance hierarchy, and drawing custom screen overlays.

04 / phantom forces support

full support for phantom forces, including full compatibility for silent aim and esp visuals.
